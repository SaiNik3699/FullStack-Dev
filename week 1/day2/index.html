About me

I am a software engineer with three-plus years developing web and enterprise applications using Java and JEE on a microservices architecture. Most of my work is Spring Boot, Hibernate and REST APIs — designing them, building them, and writing the JUnit tests that keep them honest.

I work across the full SDLC — analysis, design, implementation and testing — in Scrum teams practising test-driven development. I lean on OOP fundamentals, because they are what keep a service readable a year later.

The idea that reframed how I build things was the client is never trustworthy — every rule that matters belongs on the server.
